Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 28, 2026

Welcome to PuckGuessr ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through this Privacy Policy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit puckguessr.com, how it is used, and your choices regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

We keep data collection to the absolute minimum necessary to run the game:

  • Local Storage Data: We utilise your browser's localStorage to save your preferences and gameplay state locally on your device. This includes:
    • Your dark mode display preference
    • A flag indicating whether you have seen the welcome/first-visit popup
    • Your game progress tracking, such as your Endless mode streak and a record of player IDs you have already encountered in Endless mode to prevent immediate repetition.
  • Aggregated Gameplay Statistics: When you complete or forfeit a daily or endless puzzle, your score/guess count (e.g., win on guess 3, or a loss/forfeit marked as "X") is submitted anonymously to our backend and recorded in our aggregated database statistics. This data contains no personal identifiers, names, email addresses, or IP addresses.
  • Basic Infrastructure Traffic Data: Our website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages and utilises Cloudflare infrastructure. Standard network traffic logs (such as aggregate country-of-origin metrics) are handled via Cloudflare's standard routing network. Workers logs and individual request traces are explicitly disabled.

2. How We Use Information

The data we store or process is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To maintain your game session, streaks, and preferences across visits.
  • To prevent duplicate players from appearing sequentially in Endless mode.
  • To update aggregate, anonymous score distribution statistics for each hockey player.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

  • No Tracking Cookies: We do not use advertising cookies, third-party tracking pixels, or cross-site analytics tools (such as Google Analytics).
  • Local Storage: We use browser localStorage solely for functional gameplay features (like dark mode and streaks). You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings, though doing so will reset your active streaks and game history.

4. Third-Party Services

  • NHL API: The game fetches public player details and statistics dynamically from the official NHL Web API (api-web.nhle.com). No user personal data is sent to or shared with the NHL.
  • Cloudflare: Our site and serverless backend worker are hosted on Cloudflare infrastructure, which processes web requests in accordance with Cloudflare's privacy practices.

5. Children's Privacy

PuckGuessr is a general audience hockey trivia game and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, comments, or concerns about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out to us via our official X account linked on the website: @puckguessr.
